# Team 293 Time

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Desktop (JavaFX). This is a native desktop kiosk, not a website or mobile app.

## Users

FRC Team 293 members entering and leaving team meetings. Members type a numeric member ID using the keyboard.

## Product Purpose

Record attendance and accumulated meeting time in Google Sheets. Preserve the existing check-in/check-out workflow while making it easier to read and use.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep Google Sheets, configurable numeric IDs, keyboard input, and full-screen operation.
- Registration collects first name, last name, and email only. Do not collect ethnicity, role, or gender.
- Retain compatibility with existing attendance sheets without automatically deleting historical data.
- OAuth credentials, tokens, runtime configuration, and histories belong outside source control and release artifacts.
- Keep original MIT attribution.

## Brand Commitments

FRC Team 293, SPIKE. Official logo sourced from the team's website; gold, charcoal, and silver identity.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing JavaFX application and Google Sheets integration. Team identity verified against FIRST's team directory and the official team website. No team credentials or live attendance data supplied.
